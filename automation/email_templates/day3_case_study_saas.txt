## The Problem

A B2B SaaS company was running ads on Google, LinkedIn, and Meta — but they had no unified tracking. Each platform's dashboard told a different story. They couldn't answer the basic question: "Which channel is actually driving sign-ups?"

## What We Built

- **Unified Performance Dashboard** — Pulled data from every ad platform, Google Analytics, and their CRM into one real-time view
- **Cross-Channel Attribution** — Traced every sign-up back to its original source, not just the last click
- **KPI Framework** — Defined what actually mattered (not vanity metrics) and automated reporting
- **Weekly Automated Reports** — Stakeholders got a clean executive summary every Monday — no manual work

## The Result

- 📊  Attribution clarity: "we have no idea" → "we know exactly what works"
- 📈  Conversion rate: 4.1% → 5.0% (**23% improvement**) by reallocating budget to what actually worked
- 💰  Ad spend efficiency: Same budget, 23% more sign-ups
- 📋  Reporting time: 2 days/month → automated (0 hours)

## The Insight

They didn't need more tools. They needed their tools to work together. That's what marketing intelligence infrastructure is — not more software, but the connective tissue between what you already have.
