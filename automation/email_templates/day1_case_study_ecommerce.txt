## The Problem

An e-commerce brand was spending 15+ hours per week managing product photos, videos, and social media content. Their assets were scattered across Google Drive, Dropbox, and individual team members' computers. They had no system — just folders everywhere.

## What We Built

- **Digital Asset Management (DAM)** — Centralized all assets with proper taxonomy, tagging, and version control
- **Multi-Endpoint Delivery** — One content source feeds every channel (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, email, website) with automatic formatting per platform
- **Automated Workflows** — Content goes from approved to published without manual hand-offs

## The Result

- ⏱️  Content production time: 15 hours/week → 3 hours/week (**80% reduction**)
- 🎯  Time-to-publish: 2 days → 2 hours
- 👥  Team could focus on strategy instead of file management
- 📈  More consistent posting across all channels → 23% more organic reach

The infrastructure paid for itself in the first month from time savings alone.
